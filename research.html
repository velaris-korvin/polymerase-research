The Polymerase Research Group focuses on two flagship programmes. We do not work on small problems.

1. The β-parameter

Status: Under review at Astronomy & Astrophysics.

A new kinematic classifier for galaxy evolution, validated on 12,468 ALFALFA galaxies. Resolves Simpson's paradox between gas fraction and stellar mass. Provides the first direct observational test of the Medium framework.

2. Metabolism of the Medium

Status: Part 1 in preparation. Target: Physical Review D.

A unified theoretical framework that derives fundamental particle masses, coupling constants, and gravitational dynamics from a single substance — the Medium — governed by three metabolic equations. Nine masses from three inputs. Zero free parameters. Accuracy < 0.5%. Full research plan available on the Plan page.
