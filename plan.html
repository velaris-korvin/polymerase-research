METABOLISM OF THE MEDIUM

General Research Plan

S. Velaris Korvin
The Polymerase Research Group

The Metabolism of the Medium is a unified theoretical framework that derives the observed spectrum of elementary particles, fundamental constants, and gravitational dynamics from a single substance — the Medium — governed by three metabolic equations. The programme is structured as a series of eight publications, each addressing a distinct domain of physics while building on the results of the previous parts.

Part 1: The Medium — Axioms, Equations, and First Consequences

Status: In preparation. Target: Physical Review D.

What is introduced:

  • The Medium as a unitary substance with two projections.
  • The fundamental asymmetry Δ = 0.022, derived from QED vacuum polarisation.
  • Three metabolic equations: Gate, Accumulator, Recycler.
  • First consequences: Newtonian gravity, Klein–Gordon equation, quantum mechanics.
  • The regulator triangle geometry.
  • Derived constants: fine-structure constant, Weinberg angle.
  • Mass spectrum: π⁺, K⁺, proton, neutron, W, Z, Higgs — from three inputs, zero free parameters.
  • Experimental signatures: heliopause frequencies (Voyager), THz DNA resonances, quantum optical anomalies.
  • One falsifiable prediction.

  • D, B, J/ψ, Υ mesons and their connection to modified nucleotide bases.
  • Charm, strangeness, and bottomness as epigenetic modifications of the Medium.
  • Predicted masses for unobserved resonances.

Key Features of the Programme

  • Three inputs: the masses of the electron, muon, and tau lepton.
  • Zero free parameters after fixing the three lepton masses.
  • Nine derived quantities in Part 1 alone, with more in subsequent parts.
  • Mean accuracy < 0.5% relative to experimental values.
  • Falsifiable predictions at every stage — from sub-millimetre gravity to THz spectroscopy to neutrino astronomy.
  • All data public. All methods reproducible. All predictions testable.
  • First validated result: The β-parameter article, a direct observational test of the framework, is currently under review at Astronomy & Astrophysics.

The Medium does not replace known physics. It reveals the metabolic cycle from which known physics emerges.
