METABOLISM OF THE MEDIUM

General Research Plan

S. Velaris Korvin
The Polymerase Research Group

The Metabolism of the Medium is a unified theoretical framework that derives the observed spectrum of elementary particles, fundamental constants, and gravitational dynamics from a single substance — the Medium — governed by three metabolic equations. The programme is structured as a series of eight publications.

Part 1: The Medium — Axioms, Equations, and First Consequences

Status: In preparation. Target: Physical Review D.

A unified framework based on a single substance — the Medium. One fundamental parameter. Three metabolic equations. Nine particle masses derived from three inputs. Zero free parameters. Accuracy < 0.5%. Gravity, quantum mechanics, and the Klein–Gordon equation emerge as first consequences. One falsifiable prediction. Multiple experimental signatures.

Parts 2–8

Status: In preparation.

Extension of the framework to the full hadron spectrum, the geometry of the hidden S³ projection, tensor gravity and thermodynamics, cosmology and dark matter, neutrino physics, biophysics, and open problems. Each part builds on the previous one and provides falsifiable predictions at every stage.

Key Features of the Programme

  • Three inputs: m_e, m_μ, m_τ.
  • Zero free parameters.
  • Nine derived quantities in Part 1 alone.
  • Mean accuracy < 0.5% relative to experimental values.
  • Falsifiable predictions at every stage.
  • All data public. All methods reproducible. All predictions testable.
  • First validated result: The β-parameter article, a direct observational test of the framework, is currently under review at Astronomy & Astrophysics.

The Medium does not replace known physics. It reveals the metabolic cycle from which known physics emerges.
