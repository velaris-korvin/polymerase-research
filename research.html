The Fabric Theory

A unified framework deriving fundamental particle masses and coupling constants from three metabolic equations with a single free parameter Δ = 0.022. Predictions for lepton, meson, and baryon masses match experimental values within 0.5%.

The β-parameter

A new kinematic classifier for galaxy evolution validated on 12,468 ALFALFA galaxies. Simpson's paradox between gas fraction and stellar mass is resolved.

Cosmo-biological Isomorphism

Structural and dynamic correspondence between the Milky Way's architecture and DNA's hierarchical organisation. Based on FRB catalogue cross-correlations.

Corvin Architecture
