Polymerase Research Group is an independent, self-funded research initiative founded and led by S. Velaris Korvin. We investigate fundamental questions at the interface of astrophysics, quantum biology, and complex systems.

Our work spans observational cosmology, theoretical physics (derivation of particle masses from first principles), quantum biology (THz anomalies in biomolecules), and quantum computing architectures.

Polymerase Research Group has no institutional affiliation. All research is conducted independently. All data and code are publicly archived on Zenodo. We believe science belongs to everyone.
